# Patina, watch-only test version

## What it is

Patina shows what a Base wallet is worth, what you paid for what's in it, and how it's done. This version only watches: you paste an address and it reads what's public. It's an early test build, so expect rough edges.

## Installing it

1. Open the `.dmg`.
2. Drag Patina to Applications.
3. Open it from Applications. It lives in the menu bar, and its window opens too. If your menu bar is full, look under its "show hidden items" control.

## The invite

Patina reads prices and history from its server, which only answers people it invited. The first time you open it, its welcome screen asks for the invite you were sent. Paste it and press Continue. It's saved on this Mac, for that server alone. Please don't share it: it's yours, and it can be taken back. To change it later, go to Settings > General. To see the welcome again, choose it from the Help menu.

## What it can't do yet

It can't hold a key, sign or send. It has no Spending Money, Buy, Send or sweeps, it doesn't connect to sites or hardware wallets, and the browser extension doesn't work with it. Never send money to an address because this app showed it.

Answers you give about where something came from stay on this Mac. They're only sent to Patina's server to work out your numbers, and it keeps nothing. Prices cover ETH and a short list of tokens. Anything else shows as unknown, and history starts in November 2026.

## Problems

If the server's down, Patina says so, and it never shows you zero balances. Updates start again by themselves when it's back.

To report anything else, message the person who sent you this, with what you did, what you saw, and a screenshot if you can. Please leave your wallet address out of it unless you're happy to share it.
